CRIMSON
EXHIBITION

Tokyo, 2026. A mysterious exhibition opens its doors. Those who enter may never return unchanged.

THE STORY

In the heart of Tokyo, an exhibition appears without warning. No advertisements. No history. No artist. Only a crimson invitation.


People enter searching for art. They discover something far more dangerous. Every room reveals memories, nightmares, and secrets that were never meant to exist.

CHARACTERS

THE SANSKARI SQUAD

Four friends. Four powers. One battle that will change everything.

KAVI

POWER: INK

The main protagonist of the Sanskari Squad. Kavi wields the mysterious power of Ink and fights with his Ink Blade. His awakened Infinity Eye glows blue inside his left eye.

JAGRIT

POWER: THUNDER

A powerful Thunder user with incredible energy. Jagrit fights with speed and lightning, surrounded by intense yellow-gold electricity.

DIVYANSH

POWER: ARCHERY

An expert Archer and trusted member of the squad. Divyansh provides precise long-range support and brings skill and focus to every battle.

SHIVA

POWER: NATURE • EARTH

A Nature and Earth user who can channel powerful green energy. Shiva combines strength with his connection to the natural world.
